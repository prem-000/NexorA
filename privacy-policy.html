NexorA Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

NexorA ("the App") is a peer-to-peer communication application developed by Premsai.

1. Information We Collect

NexorA does not operate a backend server or database for collecting or storing users' personal information.

The App does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or other personal information for storage on a developer-controlled server.

2. Peer-to-Peer Communication

NexorA is designed to enable communication between supported nearby devices using device-to-device communication technologies.

Information exchanged between devices is transmitted to the intended peer as part of the communication functionality of the App. The developer does not operate a central server that stores these communications.

3. Device Permissions

Depending on the device and Android version, NexorA may request permissions required for features such as nearby-device discovery, Bluetooth communication, network connectivity, and related device-to-device communication functionality.

These permissions are used to provide the application's core communication features.

4. Local Data

NexorA may temporarily process or store information locally on the device as necessary for the application's functionality. The developer does not remotely collect this locally processed data.

5. Third-Party Services

NexorA does not intentionally use advertising, analytics, or tracking services to collect personal information from users.

6. Data Security

Because NexorA is designed for peer-to-peer communication, users should only communicate with devices and people they trust. No method of electronic communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

NexorA does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users for storage on a developer-controlled server.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since NexorA does not maintain a central server database containing user personal information, the developer does not retain such information on its servers.

Users can remove locally stored application data by using the Android device's application settings or by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or NexorA, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the NexorA application listing.
